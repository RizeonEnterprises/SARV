About Sarv

One World | One Heart

Who We Are

"Sarv" means all, complete, whole—a vision where no one is invisible, unheard, or left behind.

Sarv Foundation is a Delhi NCR–registered not-for-profit organization focused on nutrition, health, dignity, and empowerment of vulnerable communities.

Our interventions are preventive, grassroots-led, and designed with transparency to create meaningful, long-term behavioral change.

Legal & Compliance

  • 12A Registered
  • 80G Certified (Tax Exemption)
  • DARPAN Registered
  • CSR-Compliant (Active Bank Account)

Our Vision

To build a self-sustained, compassionate ecosystem where education, awareness, and wellness uplift every life, especially women and children.

Our Mission

  • To ensure every girl child learns, dreams, and leads.
  • To support women toward financial and emotional independence.
  • To promote community well-being through education and digital literacy.
