Our Essence

"Sarv" means all, complete, whole—a vision where no one is invisible.

Founded by Aamna Singh and Sri Gurparsad Singh, Sarv stands on decades of on-ground experience in education, women empowerment, and dignity-based service.

Our story began in modest community kitchens. From serving fresh rotis to teaching digital basics, Sarv evolved naturally through trust and love. This is not just an organization. It is a journey of hearts.
