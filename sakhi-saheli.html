CSR PROPOSAL

Sakhi Saheli

Menstrual Health, Awareness & Dignity Initiative
(Delhi NCR)

Project Overview

Sakhi Saheli is a women-centric menstrual health initiative combining awareness sessions with access to safe menstrual hygiene products for adolescent girls and women from underprivileged communities.

Project Rationale

Despite growing awareness around menstrual health, research insights continue to highlight a persistent gap in access, sustained education, and dignity-led engagement.

Sakhi Saheli responds to this gap by integrating:

  • Awareness & Education
  • Access to Safe Hygiene Products
  • Community Trust & Behavioral Change
