Sakhi Saheli

Menstrual Health, Awareness & Dignity Initiative
(Delhi NCR)

The Challenge

Despite growing awareness, there remains a persistent gap in access to sustained education and dignity-led engagement for adolescent girls in underserved communities.

Sakhi Saheli responds to this gap by combining:

  • Awareness Sessions
  • Access to Safe Hygiene Products
  • Community Trust & Behavioral Change

Project Overview

A women-centric menstrual health initiative combining awareness sessions with access to safe menstrual hygiene products for adolescent girls and women from underprivileged communities in Delhi NCR.
